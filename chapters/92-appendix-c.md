# Appendix C: specifications, care and safety {.nonum}

## Specifications

| | |
|---|---|
| Keys | 61 (5 octaves), touch response: initial and after touch |
| Polyphony | 16 notes |
| Sound source | iPD (Interactive Phase Distortion), 8 modules (DCO + DCA), each ON/OFF |
| Modes | Normal / Combination / Operation Memory / Multi Channel |
| Memory | Preset voices 64; internal voices 64; ROM card voices 128; RAM card voices 64. Preset operations 64 (system initialised); internal operations 64; ROM card operations 128; RAM card operations 64. Compare/Recall 5 |
| Display | 64 × 96 dot matrix, backlit |
| Inputs/outputs | Line out (1, 2, mix): impedance 0.1 kΩ, 0.6 V RMS max; headphones; foot volume; sustain; foot VR; program number; MIDI IN/OUT/THRU |
| Power | 100 V, 120 V, 220 V, 240 V AC; 16 W |
| Memory backup | Built-in lithium battery, about 5 years |
| Dimensions | 1060 (W) × 324 (D) × 93 (H) mm |
| Weight | 12 kg (26.5 lbs) |
| Accessories | AC cord, ROM card, plug cord set, dust cover |

Design and specifications are subject to change without notice.

## Care

1. Avoid heat, humidity and direct sunlight.
2. Avoid severe impacts; pack carefully (use a case).
3. Keep liquids, dirt, dust and metallic objects out of the keys.
4. Never modify the unit; take faults to an authorised Casio service centre.
5. Clean with a soft cloth and mild detergent; no thinner, alcohol or benzene.
6. Keep away from televisions and radios (digital circuitry can interfere).

**ROM card (RC-100):** do not bend or drop; store in its case; do not disassemble; Casio is not responsible for losses of data.

## Safety

* Read all instructions; supervise children; keep away from water, heat sources; use only a recommended stand.
* High volumes (with amplifier or headphones) can cause permanent hearing loss.
* Ensure ventilation; connect only to the type of supply marked on the unit.
* Unplug the cord during long periods of non-use; do not spill liquids or drop objects into it.
* Have it serviced by qualified personnel if the cord/plug is damaged, liquid/objects entered, it was exposed to rain, doesn't operate normally, or was dropped.
* **Grounding:** the unit must be grounded; use an appropriate grounded outlet; do not modify the plug.
* **FCC (U.S.A.):** tested and complies with Class B computing device limits (Part 15 Subpart J). If it causes interference: reorient/relocate the antenna or equipment, or use a different outlet circuit.
* Changing the voltage selector may require a different cord or plug; refer it to qualified personnel.
